<div data-type="detailGroup" class="isomer-accordion isomer-accordion-white">
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>2015 – 2016</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>The Ministry of Health (MOH) approved the establishment of a national
cell therapy facility to be sited at the National Cancer Centre Singapore
(NCCS).</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>2017 – 2018</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>The National Cell Therapy Centre Implementation Office (NCTC IO) was set
up to oversee the development of cell therapy services in Singapore and
the operationalisation of the National Cell Therapy Facility (CTF) under
the working name National Cell Therapy Centre (NCTC).</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>2019 – 2020</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>NCTC evolved into ACTRIS, a programme of the Consortium for Clinical Research
and Innovation, Singapore (CRIS), and the design and fit-out works for
ACTRIS commenced.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>2021 – 2022</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>Despite disruptions from the global pandemic, ACTRIS advanced the development
of its facility and expanded its skilled workforce.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>2023 – 2024</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>ACTRIS was officially launched by Mr Ong Ye Kung,&nbsp;Minister for Health
and Coordinating Minister for Social Policies.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The first investigational cell therapy product, an autologous dendritic
cell vaccine for hepatocellular carcinoma, was manufactured at ACTRIS for
a clinical trial at NCCS.</p>
<p></p>
<p>ACTRIS and A*STAR’s Bioprocessing Technology Institute (BTI) established
the Process Accelerator for Cell Therapy Manufacturing (PACTMAN) programme
to deepen Singapore’s process development capabilities.</p>
</div>
</details>
<details class="isomer-details">
<summary><strong>2025</strong>
</summary>
<div data-type="detailsContent" class="isomer-details-content">
<p>ACTRIS received its Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) Certification for
Manufacturing of Investigational Finished Products and Manufacturer’s Licence
for Finished Products for Specially Authorised Clinical Use.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The first clinical service product, an autologous chimeric antigen receptor
T-cell for the treatment of blood cancer, was manufactured by ACTRIS for
use under the Innovative Salvage Therapy framework at the National University
Cancer Institute, Singapore (NCIS).</p>
</div>
</details>
</div>
