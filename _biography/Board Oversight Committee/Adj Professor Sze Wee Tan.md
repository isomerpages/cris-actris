<h4>Profile</h4>
<p><strong>Education and Training :</strong> Professor Tan graduated with
MBBS from <strong>National University of Singapore (NUS)</strong>, and attained
his MBA from <strong>Warwick University, UK</strong>. He attended the Stanford
Executive Programme at the <strong>Stanford University’s Graduate School Business</strong>,
and was awarded a certificate of completion for the Senior Fellowship in
Public Service Programme, <strong>Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy</strong> in
Singapore. Prof Tan also attended the Executive Leadership Programme from
the <strong>Singapore Civil Service College</strong>. He is an affiliate
member of the <strong>Faculty of Pharmaceutical Medicine, Royal College of Physicians, UK</strong>.
He is a registered medical practitioner with the <strong>Singapore Medical Council</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Leadership Appontment :</strong> Prof Tan Sze Wee, is currently
Advisor to <strong>Temasek Singapore Pte Ltd</strong>. Temasek is a global
investment company headquartered in Singapore with a multinational staff
of over 900 people. Prof Tan will be supporting Temasek at <strong>BioSparc</strong> in
Suzhou. This is a China-Singapore Life Sciences Park, which Temasek is
working on with Suzhou Industrial Park. It aims to build on the SIP’s strengths
in the biomedical sector, providing research and development centres and
manufacturing facilities to advance cutting-edge innovations from drug
development and medical devices to artificial intelligence-driven medical
technologies.</p>
<p>Between 2009 to 2025, Prof Tan served various leadership roles in the <strong>Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A*STAR)</strong>,
which is Singapore's lead public sector R&amp;D agency. As a Science and
Technology Organisation, A*STAR bridges the gap between academia and industry.
A*STAR's R&amp;D activities span biomedical sciences to physical sciences
and engineering, with over 6,000 staff within 17 research entities and
national platforms primarily located in Biopolis and Fusionopolis.</p>
<p>Prof Tan’s career in A*STAR started in 2009. He was first appointed as <strong>Programme Director of the A*STAR Medical Technology initiative</strong> in
Science and Engineering Research Council (SERC), that led in the partnership
with <strong>Stanford BioDesign @ Stanford University</strong>, and <strong>Center for Integration of Medicine &amp; Innovative Technology (CIMIT)</strong> in
Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston, USA. Prof Tan was subsequently
appointed as Director (SERC), where he oversaw all SERC cluster-led research
and development (R&amp;D). From 2012 to 2015, Prof Tan was <strong>Deputy Executive Director (DED), Biomedical Research Council (BMRC) </strong>and
his cross-Council experience enabled A*STAR to engage companies across
industry sectors.</p>
<p>In 2016, Prof Tan was appointed ED, SERC and was subsequently redesignated
as <strong>Assistant Chief Executive (ACE), SERC</strong> in 2019. As ACE
SERC, Prof Tan drove the implementation of strategic plans and initiatives
set for A*STAR in the Science and Engineering areas. Prof Tan was appointed <strong>ACE, Innovation and Enterprise as well as Acting Chief Executive of A*ccelerate Technologies Pte Ltd</strong> in
2020. He aligned A*STAR industry engagement, licensing and venture creation
value chain among the A*STAR Research Institutes and the National Platforms.
This is to drive efforts to translate A*STAR’s R&amp;D to market so as
to enable research-fuelled economic and societal impact.</p>
<p>From 2023 to 2025, Prof Tan was appointed <strong>ACE BMRC</strong>. BMRC
supports, oversees and coordinates public sector biomedical research and
development activities in Singapore to cultivate a thriving biomedical
cluster. At BMRC, he oversaw the development of core research capabilities
in bioprocessing, genomics and proteomics, molecular and cell biology,
bioengineering and nanotechnology, and computational biology. Prof Tan
also served on the governance committee of National Platforms <strong>Diagnostic Development Hub </strong>(DxDHub),<strong> Experimental Drug Development Centre</strong> (EDDC)
and <strong>Medtech Catapult</strong>.</p>
<p>Prior to these roles, Prof Tan was the Managing Director and Chief Executive
Officer of <strong>Rockeby biomed Limited</strong> from 2001 to 2009. He
was the Asia-Pacific Associate Regional Medical Director for <strong>Mead Johnson Nutritional’s, a division pf Bristol-Myers Squibb Company</strong> from
1997 to 2001.</p>
<p><strong>Academic, Clinical and Innovation Focus :</strong> Professor Tan
constantly promotes international research, innovation and enterprise partnership
between Singapore, A*STAR and its key scientific, industry and venture
building partners across the world. He has forged bilateral research partnership
programmes between <strong>Singapore and China, Germany, Korea, Japan, New Zealand</strong>.
He is a speaker at international conferences on topics around medical technologies,
AI in Healthtech, and advises academic, corporate and not for profit organisations
for wide range of topics. Within A*STAR, he oversees close to 2,000 research
scientists and engineers during his stint at BMRC and SERC, each with annual
operating budget of US$300M.</p>
<p><strong>Professional Service:</strong> Professor Tan has served on various
national committees in Singapore. He is a member of the<strong> Board Oversight Committee</strong> for <strong>Advanced Cell Therapy and Research Institute, Singapore (ACTRIS)</strong>.
He is a board member of the <strong>Singapore Eye Research Institute (SERI)</strong> as
well as a member of its<strong> Incubator Advisory Board for the SERI Ophthalmic Technologies Incubator</strong> Phase
II Program. He is a council member of the <strong>Singapore Medical Association (SMA)</strong>,
as well as a board director for <strong>SMA Pte Ltd</strong>. Prof Tan is
also the <strong>Chairman of Unlocking ADHD Ltd</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Awards &amp; Honours : </strong>Professor Tan was a former <strong>Nominated Member of Parliament of Singapore</strong>,
and is a recipient of the <strong>Singapore Public Administration Medal (Silver)</strong> as
well as the <strong>Singapore (Covid-19) Public Administration Medal (Silver)</strong>.
He has also received the Friend of Labour Award.
