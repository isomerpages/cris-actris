clinicians, scientists, researchers and start-up companies to <strong>join a half-day workshop</strong> exploring<strong> End-to-end Product and Project Development &amp; Execution at ACTRIS</strong>.</p>
<p>Gain practical insights into project readiness assessment, engagement

cell and gene therapy (CGT) products towards clinical translation.</p>
